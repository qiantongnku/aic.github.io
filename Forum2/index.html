    2026 International Post-Conference Workshop on Artificial Intelligence and Computing (Post-AIC 2026)

    The Post-AIC 2026 – South Korea Extension Workshop is an official follow-up event affiliated with the inaugural 1st International Conference on Artificial Intelligence and Computing (AIC 2026). Designed to deepen the impact of the main conference, this workshop will take place in two world-class South Korean destinations from May 6–14, 2026.

    Itinerary & Venues

    • May 6 – 9, 2026: Technical Sessions and Academic Forums in Ansan City.
    • May 10 – 14, 2026: Strategic Networking and Collaborative Workshops at the Sun Cruise Resort and Yacht (950-39 Heonhwa-ro, Gangdong-myeon, Gangneung).

    Workshop Mission

    Post-AIC 2026 provides a high-level international forum for innovative academics and industrial experts to further explore the breakthroughs presented at the main conference. By shifting the focus from initial presentation to in-depth analysis and collaborative synthesis, the workshop serves as a vital platform for:

    • Deep-Dive Discussions: Expanding on the AI theories and applications debuted during AIC 2026.
    • Project Incubation: Facilitating scholarly exchange and academic collaboration to turn conference insights into actionable research projects.
    • Strategic Partnerships: Giving researchers and industry leaders a unique opportunity to solidify new partnerships in a refined, immersive environment.

    Setting the stage for the future of AI development, this post-conference event ensures that the momentum of the inaugural AIC 2026 translates into lasting global impact.
